# Business model — working draft

**Status:** Draft; validate with product owner.

## Participants
- Customer
- Rental company / vehicle supplier
- Independent driver
- Platform administrator / operations
- Payment provider

## Booking modes
### Self-drive
Customer rents an eligible vehicle and drives it. Driver selection is not part
of this booking mode. Define license, age, deposit, insurance, and verification
rules before implementation.

### Vehicle + driver
Customer rents a vehicle and separately selects an eligible independent driver.
The vehicle supplier is not assumed to employ or provide the driver. The
platform must validate both resources and their availability.

## Driver marketplace
Potential capabilities to validate:
- driver registration and identity/document verification
- license and qualification checks where legally required
- profile, service area, languages, vehicle/class eligibility if relevant
- availability calendar and working hours
- rates, fees, cancellation policy, and payout account
- customer choice and explainable recommendation ranking
- reviews, complaints, incident handling, suspension, and reactivation
- trip lifecycle, earnings ledger, commissions, and payout reconciliation

## Questions to resolve
1. Which countries, cities, and jurisdictions launch first?
2. Who owns the customer contract: platform, supplier, or both?
3. Who sets vehicle and driver prices? Can the platform add fees?
4. Is the driver hired for the entire rental or only transfers/segments?
5. Can customers bring their own driver?
6. What are minimum/maximum rental durations and booking lead times?
7. What are cancellation, refund, deposit, no-show, and damage policies?
8. Which party is responsible for insurance and incident management?
9. How are driver identity, licenses, background checks, and documents verified?
10. What is the commission and payout model for suppliers and drivers?
11. Which payment methods, currencies, taxes, and invoicing rules apply?
12. What are the admin dispute, moderation, and emergency workflows?
